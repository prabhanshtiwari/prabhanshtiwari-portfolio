Bachelor of Computer Applications.

I completed my BCA at United University, Prayagraj, in 2026. It became the formal starting point for my journey into programming, application development and computer science.

United University · PrayagrajCompleted · 2026

01 / THE DECISION

Why I chose BCA

I wanted an undergraduate path centred on computers and software. BCA gave me a structured place to start learning how applications are designed and built, while helping me move from being a technology user to someone who wants to understand the systems behind it.

02 / WHAT THE JOURNEY BUILT

From concepts to practical thinking

The most valuable part of the degree was developing a foundation for continued learning: breaking problems into smaller steps, understanding how data is stored and processed, and connecting programming concepts to working applications.

ProgrammingLogic, problem-solving and writing code

What I carry forward

BCA was the starting point, not the finish line. It gave me a reason to keep exploring web development, backend systems and cloud technologies through projects, independent study and postgraduate learning.
