EDUCATION / 02

Master of Computer Applications.

I’m continuing my academic journey with an online MCA from Chandigarh University, building on my BCA while continuing to learn through practical development work.

Chandigarh UniversityOnline programme · In progress

01 / THE DECISION

Why I chose an online MCA

After completing BCA, I wanted to continue into postgraduate computer applications without stepping away from practical work, projects and independent learning. The online format offers the flexibility to organise study alongside those commitments, while keeping a structured academic programme in the picture.

02 / WHY CHANDIGARH UNIVERSITY

A structured next step

I chose Chandigarh University for my online MCA because I wanted a defined postgraduate learning path that I could pursue remotely. The format is important to me: I can revisit academic material, manage my learning schedule and keep applying concepts through hands-on projects.

This page describes my personal reasons for choosing the programme; it is not a claim about comparative rankings or guaranteed outcomes.

03 / WHAT I WANT TO DEEPEN

Connect the theory with real systems

My focus is to strengthen computer science fundamentals and apply them in software that people can actually use. I want to understand not only how to build a feature, but also the data structures, databases, operating-system concepts, networks, architecture and engineering trade-offs behind it.

Computer science foundationsDeeper reasoning about core concepts
Software engineeringDesigning, testing and maintaining applications
Data & analyticsWorking with structured data and useful insights
Cloud & deploymentUnderstanding how systems run beyond a laptop

04 / MY APPROACH

Study, build, explain, repeat

I don’t want the degree to remain only an academic exercise. I plan to connect what I study with small experiments, documented projects and explanations that make the underlying ideas easier to understand.
